01 · problem

Finance is drowning in bots.

Management cannot see or trust what those bots publish. Nobody can trace a conclusion to data, method, and human judgment - and the analyst may not be human.

  • Bot sprawl Agents draft flash, forecast, and ops notes faster than review can keep up.
  • Un-auditable publish The artifact is a doc or sheet - not a package with provenance.
  • Spreadsheet archaeology Next week starts from buried files, not a prior sounding.

Say: bots are live; trust and trace are not.

02 · solution

Every publish is a complete sounding.

An Analysis Package is the unit of work - then a gate, then a chart room.

  • Package Pinned inputs, method, engines, human overrides with credit.
  • Gate Structural CI before it counts as published.
  • Chart room Managers query the team's validated corpus with citations.

Not another planning monolith. The publish contract - and the room to question it.

Say: package, gate, chart room.

03 · why now

Three unlocks landed together.

  • 01 · Bots are already live Finance teams already run agent sprawl. The liability is here.
  • 02 · CI on the publish unit Structure can finally attach to the analysis package - not only warehouse tables.
  • 03 · LLMs after structure Corpus Q&A and reports work when packages are real - not on chat sludge.

Say: agents first, structure second, intelligence third.

04 · insight

why this shape

Real plans need massive unstructured data and judgment. They will never look like a clean structured pipeline or a traditional planning tool alone.

That judgment is the product surface - labels, truths, overrides - with credit. Not an afterthought bolted on a warehouse.

Say: judgment is load-bearing; we make it the unit of record.
