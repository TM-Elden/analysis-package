01 · Logo

Primary wordmark — the sunk vowel

Lowercase Archivo, condensed width, heavy weight. The "a" drops .19em below the baseline and takes the brass accent — one letter, sitting deeper than the rest.

Primary · dark backgrounds
fathm
Font: Archivo · wght 680 · wdth 84
Case: always lowercase
Sunk vowel: translateY(.19em) · #E8B36A
Clearspace: height of the "f" on all sides
Inverse · light backgrounds
fathm
Ink on bone. Brass vowel stays brass —
it passes contrast on both grounds.
Never: gradients, outlines, all-caps,
or un-sinking the vowel.
Secondary mark · sounding line

slide corners, loading states.
Never lockup with the wordmark side-by-side

Wordmark · small sizes

03 · Type

Type system

Three faces, three jobs. The serif is the voice; the grotesk is the workhorse; the mono is the instrument panel.

UI / Body

04 · Copy formulas

The definition device

The signature move. Every major surface — hero, feature, announcement — can open with a dictionary entry. Here's the formula and a starter lexicon.

[term] (n./v.) — [plain-spoken definition, ≤14 words, no jargon].
Rules: always lowercase · serif face · part-of-speech in brass italic · one entry per surface · definition must be true of the product, not just poetic.
fathm (v.)
to understand something fully, down to its source.
sounding (n.)
a single verified measurement. the unit of trust.
provenance (n.)
where a claim came from, and how it got here.

one structure for every analysis. enforced, not suggested.
