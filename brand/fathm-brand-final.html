The mark

One object. The letter is the instrument.

The F is a sounding line — the weighted, marked rope sailors dropped to measure what they couldn't see. Crop it and it's the icon; set it before "athm" and it's the wordmark. There is nothing to keep consistent, because there is only one thing.

  • R·01
    The arms are type.

    Top arm at cap height, second at x-height — set in bone so they shake hands with the letters that follow.

  • R·02
    The line is the instrument.

    Brass, always. It enters above the word and exits below it — the only element that crosses the baseline.

  • R·03
    The weight is the payoff.

    A round brass lead, 1.3× the stroke. It settles below the baseline: the measurement, taken.

  • R·04
    Flat forever.

    No gradients, glows, or shadows on the mark itself. Depth belongs to the environment, not the object.
