## Mitigations if we keep **fathm**

1. **Always pair with category:**  
   `fathm - analysis package provenance` / `the chart room for AI-published analysis`  
2. **Never** imply affiliation with fathom.ai, Fathom HQ, or Fathm Limited.  
3. **Logo-first** in UI (sounding-line F) so visual identity is not only the word.  
4. **Tagline lock** is ours: "to understand something fully, down to its source."  
5. **Format name stays portable:** Analysis Package (`ap`) can outlive a product rename.  
6. **Budget for counsel** before: USPTO filing, paid ads, YC public batch brand, enterprise MSA trademark warranties.  
7. **Consider dual brand:** product **fathm** + legal entity different name if word mark is hard.
