Retention & holds

Legal holds, recalled packages, and packages past the configured retention_days marker (set on the Settings screen). Nothing here deletes anything automatically - a package flagged as retention-due must be explicitly recalled or superseded before it can be purged; purge itself only runs from the package detail page, with a type-the-id confirmation, and refuses an approved or legally-held package outright.
