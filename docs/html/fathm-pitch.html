ANALYSIS PACKAGE FORMAT · CHART ROOM PRODUCT

01 · problem

Most analysis is a surface reading.

Every company doing data analysis today has the same hidden liability: nobody can reliably trace a conclusion back to the data and method that produced it. As AI agents generate more of that analysis, the liability compounds — because the analyst you cannot audit may not even be human.

  • opaque
    Conclusions float free of inputsNo durable link from number to snapshot, method, and human override.
  • untyped
    Agents publish without a packageChat and spreadsheets are the record. Provenance is reconstructed in the audit, if at all.
  • unpriced
    The liability compounds with every agent turnSame teams that need audit trails for finance and ops have none for AI-drafted work.
02 · why now

Two unlocks landed together.

LLMs make it possible to enforce standards on the content and reasoning of an analysis — not only its formatting or schema. CI could always check “is there a field called methodology.” It could never check “does this conclusion actually follow from this data” until now. That is the unlock. Agent volume makes enforcement urgent.

agents already draft

Finance, ops, and forecasting teams already use AI agents to draft analysis and reports inside real workflows.

standards can bite

A publish unit (the Analysis Package) plus CI can enforce structure now; semantic review of reasoning is the layer LLMs newly enable.

03 · wedge

the standard (n.) — one structure for every analysis. enforced, not suggested.

Buyer: finance, ops, and forecasting teams publishing via AI agents — where provenance is already a compliance or executive trust requirement and the pain is acute.

Narrow, budget-holding. Not “data analysis” broadly. Agent + Excel remain valid UI; the package is the publish artifact, not a demand to abandon spreadsheets on day one.

05 · moat

provenance (n.) — where a claim came from, and how it got here.

Not the software. A well-funded competitor or foundation model lab can replicate a schema-and-CI product in a quarter. The moat is the proprietary, standardized, provenance-tagged corpus that only accumulates by running the pipeline over real customer usage.

wedge

Software earns the right to run on real publish cycles.

asset

The data — and structure-level learning under trust constraints — is the long-term position. Closer to Bloomberg or Palantir than typical point SaaS.

06b · who wins

sounding (n.) - a single verified measurement. the unit of trust.

Success requires managers and planners. If only leadership buys, planners sandbag. fathm makes the planner’s work legible capital - not a training set for their replacement.

managers · company bot

  • Query the chart room with citations
  • Audit-complete publish by default
  • Governance on the Standard (HITL)
  • Lower risk on AI-assisted analysis

planners

  • Credit - overrides carry author, reason, evidence
  • Less thrash - prior pack + diff, not archaeology
  • Defense in the meeting - show-your-work is one link
  • Craft visible - gold packs and clean gates surface skill
  • Role moves up - judgment and truths, not dumb explode/allocate

objection · “why train ai to replace me?”

You’re not feeding a model your secret sauce so it can fire you. You’re publishing soundings so your judgment is the unit of record. Helpful AI has to show the chain back to you. Replacing you without that chain is exactly what fathm makes expensive.

Trust: no cross-customer content training; training eligibility opt-in; models draft only - humans and deterministic engines own accepted math.

Do not lead with headcount efficiency. Lead with legibility, credit, and less thrash. Pilot with named planner authorship on the first gold packs - not only manager buyers.

07 · trust

Decided. Not open.

The trust answer is the product stance, not an objection handle.

  • Train only on structure and conformance behavior — how packages get flagged and fixed
  • Never on underlying business content for any cross-customer model
  • Never pool one customer’s data into a model resold to another
  • Per-customer Team Models stay isolated to that customer’s instance

This is the only stance that lets us sell to competitors in the same industry without an immediate veto.
